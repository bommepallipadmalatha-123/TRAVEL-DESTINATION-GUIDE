Paris, France

Paris, the capital of France and nicknamed the "City of Light," is a global hub for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture located along the Seine River. As of 2025, the metropolitan area boasts a population of over 13 million, making it a major European center known for landmarks like the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, and Notre-Dame Cathedral.

Kyoto, Japan

Kyoto, the former imperial capital of Japan for over 1,000 years (794–1868), is the country's cultural and historical heart, renowned for its 17 UNESCO World Heritage sites, 2,000+ temples/shrines, and traditional atmosphere. Located in the Kansai region, this major city preserves prewar heritage, including Geisha districts (Gion), tea houses, and exquisite gardens.

New York City, USA

New York City, located at the mouth of the Hudson River in Southeastern New York State, is the most populous and influential city in the US, known for its iconic skyline, cultural diversity, and status as a global hub for finance, media, and art. Comprising five boroughs—Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, The Bronx, and Staten Island—it is a densely populated metropolis with a rich history, thriving economy, and unparalleled energy.

Delhi, India

Delhi, India's capital, is a sprawling metropolis blending ancient history with modern development, serving as India's political, cultural, and commercial heart, famous for landmarks like the Red Fort, Qutub Minar, India Gate, vibrant markets like Chandni Chowk, diverse food (Chole Bhature), and a rich history evident in its numerous monuments, though it also faces significant challenges like severe air pollution.

Sydney, Australia

Sydney is Australia's largest, oldest, and most populous city, serving as the capital of New South Wales with over 5.5 million residents. Located on the southeast coast, it is famed for its stunning natural harbor, the iconic Sydney Opera House, and the Harbour Bridge. It offers a vibrant, multicultural lifestyle with world-renowned beaches like Bondi, a thriving culinary scene, and a temperate climate.
